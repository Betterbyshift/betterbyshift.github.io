Two small routines that prevent bigger headaches

5 checks before releasing work to production

  1. Confirm approved drawings.
  2. Verify required materials.
  3. Close outstanding customer approvals.
  4. Check production capacity.
  5. Confirm quality requirements.

Use this checklist during planning, then organize ongoing readiness with the Production Readiness Tracker.

5 steps to close a meeting with accountability

  1. Write down each action.
  2. Assign one clear owner.
  3. Set a realistic due date.
  4. Record blockers and status.
  5. Review open actions next meeting.

Keep the follow-through organized with the Action Item Tracker.
